About me

Curious by nature,
deliberate by practice

I'm a product designer based in San Francisco with over seven years of experience shaping digital products across health, fintech, and enterprise software. I believe good design is invisible — it just feels right.

My work begins with listening: to users, to data, to the constraints of the problem. From there I move fast — sketching, prototyping, testing, never precious about any single idea until I know it's the right one.

I'm equally comfortable in a five-person startup sprint and a structured design-ops environment at scale. What drives me in both is the same: making complex things genuinely simple for real people.
